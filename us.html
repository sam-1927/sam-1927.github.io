My Dearest Baby,

Happy 10 months to us, my sweetest unicorn! From the very first day, you've filled my life with the exact warmth of a cozy bakery on a rainy afternoon — sweet, comforting, and impossible not to fall in love with every single day.

I still remember your eyes lighting up, bunny, when you told me about your dream of opening your own café, paired with a flower boutique and a little bookstore right next door. Until the day we cut the real ribbon on Unicorn Bakes by Samidha, I wanted to build you this little corner of the internet so you can already feel what it's like to own it.

Thank you for every laugh, every hazelnut pastry & hot chocolate craving, every late-night talk, and for being my favorite person in the whole world. Here's to 10 magical months behind us, baby, and a lifetime of baking, flowers, books, and adventures ahead.

Forever yours,

Your #1 Customer & Forever Taste-Tester
